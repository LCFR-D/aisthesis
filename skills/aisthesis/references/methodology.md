# Methodology and decision records

## Five modes

- **Greenfield:** establish a new interface contract while preserving product truth and constraints.
- **Refinement:** preserve identity, routes, behavior, and copy intent; replace only evidenced defects.
- **Redesign:** explicit permission to replace the visual or interaction system while preserving product truth and accessibility.
- **Audit:** diagnose and rank; modify nothing.
- **Study:** extract general principles from references without copying pixels, code, tokens, or protected expression.

## Evidence classes

Keep three ledgers:

1. **Deterministic:** source, DOM, image, network, console, geometry, accessibility, and performance facts.
2. **Multimodal:** localized observations about objects, materials, hierarchy, grouping, and narrative.
3. **Human:** explicit approval, rejection, or calibration examples.

A finding may link ledgers but may not merge them. “A seam exists at y=420” is measurable. “It looks pasted” is a visual observation. “The background asset causes it” is a hypothesis until source/DOM evidence confirms ownership.

## Decision record

For each material decision record:

- question and selected mode;
- higher-authority constraints;
- options considered;
- evidence for and against;
- selected choice and rejected alternatives;
- files/owners affected;
- rollback and verification.

## Provenance gate

Classify every outside influence as principle, pattern, token, asset, code, or protected expression. Record source, licence, permitted reuse, modification, and attribution. Unknown licence means exclude direct copying. It does not prevent an original solution to the underlying problem.

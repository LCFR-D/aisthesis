# Provenance

Aisthesis is an LCFR-authored, standalone frontend operating system. It consolidates the methods LCFR uses across frontend direction, implementation, evidence, and release:

- Metis: scope, product truth, authority order, operating loop, provenance, and release governance;
- Taste and craft: brief inference, subject-grounded direction, anti-template constraints, production completeness, bounded refinement, and design quality;
- product and design-system practice: journey architecture, complete states, semantic tokens, component contracts, forms, data density, and recovery;
- interaction craft: direct manipulation, feedback, focus, motion purpose, timing, interruption, and cleanup;
- Aisthesis Judgment: evidence boundaries, localized rendered-interface critique, and human calibration;
- Kinetograph and Scrolltelling QA: exact progress, native-scroll verification, physical pacing, browser state, and reproducible frame evidence;
- Brand System Development: source locking, visual grammar, semantic tokens, asset provenance, and paired modes;
- Responsive Generated Brand Art: breakpoint masters, protected regions, and responsive image delivery;
- LCFR release practice: dogfooding, accessibility, security, performance, independent review, preview parity, and production read-back.

The capability map names external and internal skill families such as Impeccable, Taste, Hallmark, Frontend Design, UI/UX pattern intelligence, and interaction design so users can understand what was absorbed.

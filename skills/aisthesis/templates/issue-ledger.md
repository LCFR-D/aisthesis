Severity:

- Critical: security/privacy failure, destructive loss, blocked primary journey, or release-integrity failure.
- High: primary journey, supported accessibility path, or major responsive/cross-browser failure.
- Medium: secondary journey or material clarity/performance failure with a workaround.
- Low: localized polish issue without task or meaning impact.

For each resolved item record the fixing revision and exact verification result. Similar screenshots are not duplicates unless cause and user impact are the same.
